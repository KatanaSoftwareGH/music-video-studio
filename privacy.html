Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Your files

Music Video Studio processes your artwork, audio and video entirely on your own device. We do not upload, store or collect them on any server.

Google and YouTube sign-in

If you choose to upload to YouTube, the app asks for the youtube.upload permission through Google Sign-In. It is used only to upload the video you exported, with the title and visibility you choose. The access token is held in memory during your session, is not stored by us, and is sent only to Google APIs. We do not receive, store, share or sell your Google account data.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local storage

The app may save preferences, such as a Google client ID you enter, in your browser local storage on your device.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
